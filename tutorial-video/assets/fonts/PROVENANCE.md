# Retained fonts

The four unmodified TrueType fonts are DejaVu 2.37, retained from Debian's
`fonts-dejavu-core` package version `2.37-8`. The project's typography therefore
does not depend on a workstation's installed fonts.

[Upstream project](https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/) ·
[upstream license](https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html).
The complete upstream font license and Debian copyright notice accompany the
fonts. Sources reviewed October 6, 2026. `assets/inventory.json` records the
retained bytes; preparation verifies them before synthesis or rendering.
